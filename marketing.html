Blood Tracking Assistant is designed to provide hunters of all levels of expertise with information to improve their wounded game recovery. Whether you're new to the woods or a mountain man, Blood Tracking Assistant aims to provide the right guidance when you need it the most!

Why Choose Blood Tracking Assistant?

  • Ease of Use: Blood Tracking Assistant's simple design allows for seamless data entry in the field.
  • Informed Decisions: Utilize tracking recommendations to improve post-shot tracking.
  • Ethical Responsibility: Promote ethical hunting practices by exercising responsible recovery practices.

Our Commitment

Empowering hunters to make responsible decisions when tracking wounded game!

The app is intended to help both new and experienced hunters by putting important tracking information in one convenient location.
