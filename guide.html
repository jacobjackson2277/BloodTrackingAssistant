Getting Started

The proposed application is designed to be simple to use in the field. The following steps explain how a hunter would use the Blood Tracking Assistant once developed.

Step 1: Begin a New Tracking Session

Open Blood Tracking Assistant and select the "New Tracking Session" button to begin analyzing your track.

Step 2: Enter Shot Information

Record shot details in the boxes labeled "shot placement," "distance," and "observed animal behavior."

Step 3: Document Blood Trail

Record blood details in the boxes labeled "blood color," "blood consistency," and "blood quantity."

Step 4: Review Recommendation

Review the application's suggested waiting period, tracking strategies, and next steps based on the information entered.

Remember to use your own judgment in the field and follow applicable hunting regulations. This is an application concept, not a completed recovery tool.
