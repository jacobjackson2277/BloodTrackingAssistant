Welcome to Blood Tracking Assistant

Every hunter is aware that a hunt is only half complete following a successful shot. Recovering wounded game can often be difficult, especially when paired with less-than-ideal shot conditions and sparse blood trails.

Blood Tracking Assistant is designed to serve hunters in these conditions by allowing hunters to record observations, analyze blood trails, and review a recommended course of action based on the details provided. By combining modern technology and hunter instinct, Blood Tracking Assistant aims to help hunters make informed decisions and promote ethical hunting!

This website presents the proposed application and its features as part of my ITCC298 capstone project.
