Our Story

In December of 2019, I encountered a young hunter at the Caddo National Grassland Wildlife Management Area. This hunter was distraught after making a shot on a whitetail deer that had become difficult to track. The hunter's lack of experience made tracking the wounded animal incredibly difficult in the remote area, and he needed guidance.

Fortunately, I was able to mentor this young hunter in that moment, and we recovered the animal after a 600-yard track. During this experience, I was able to explain the process of game recovery to the young hunter and witness the difference it made in his confidence.

In that moment, I knew my mission was to bring reliable blood tracking information to novice hunters around the world! Blood Tracking Assistant is my attempt to accomplish that mission by making ethical, reliable, and responsible tracking recommendations more accessible to anybody with a smart device.

Project creator: Jacob Jackson | ITCC298
